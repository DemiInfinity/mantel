# Code of conduct

Mantel is a small, friendly project. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background or
experience.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something isn't clear.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it the same way.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else you wouldn't say to someone's face at a friendly meet-up.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately through GitHub's **Report content** option, or the private reporting described
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer will look at every report and may edit or remove
comments, or block people who don't follow this code.
